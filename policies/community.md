# Forge community rules

Build games with people, not at their expense.

- Credit contributors, artists, translators, playtesters, and upstream projects accurately.
- Respect the license and trademark rules shown before you fork, export, or submit.
- Review the work, explain design intent, and critique specific game behavior rather than attacking people.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, dox, spam, manipulate votes, or hide conflicts of interest.
- Use content notes where a game’s subject matter reasonably calls for them.
- Maintainers must apply review and jam rules consistently, document disqualification or takedown reasons, and provide an appeal/contact path.
- Security reports, rights complaints, moderation reports, and appeals go to {{CONTACT}}.

Moderation actions are recorded in an audit trail. Serious or repeated violations may remove content or beta access.
