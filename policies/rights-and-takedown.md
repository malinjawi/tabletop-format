# Rights, attribution, and takedown process

Forge requires a machine-readable rights manifest and blocks release when a file has unknown or incompatible rights. That gate helps maintainers; it is not a legal determination.

Send reports to {{CONTACT}} with: your name and contact details; the exact Forge project/release/file URL; the copyrighted or protected work; the basis for your authority; the requested action; and a good-faith accuracy statement. Do not send passwords or unrelated personal data.

Forge will acknowledge the report, preserve the relevant immutable identifiers, restrict access when warranted, notify the project owner when lawful, record the action, and provide an appeal/contact path. Current branches may be corrected, while already distributed copies and Git history may require de-indexing or a superseding release rather than silent rewriting.

For security vulnerabilities, send a minimal reproduction privately to {{CONTACT}} and allow reasonable remediation time before public disclosure.
