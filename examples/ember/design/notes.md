# Design notes — Ember

*The designer diary. Prose that diffs; future contributors read this to
understand not just what the game is, but why.*

## Why 14 cards

The whole game must fit in a pocket and teach itself in one game. Every card
must earn its slot three ways: a use when ahead, a use when behind, and a
reason to draft it. Kindling is the floor everything is measured against.

## The ash economy

Ash is the game's memory: aggression today becomes resources tomorrow. It
exists to make losing a clash *interesting* — the loser gains tempo toward
Wildfire and Ash Cloak lines. If ash ever feels like bookkeeping instead of
a comeback engine, it gets cut.

## Why restrict Wildfire instead of nerfing it

Playtests (2026-06-20, 2026-06-27) showed the card itself is beloved — the
problem was *consistency* of the double-Wildfire opening, not the ceiling.
Restriction preserves the dream; a cost nerf would have killed the burn
archetype entirely. This is a values call: keep cards scary, limit access.
