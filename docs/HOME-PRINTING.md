# Home printing

Open **Design → Configure print** to save a repeatable print recipe. Review the settings and commit them before downloading. Print downloads use committed source, including the selected card quantities and artwork.

## Choose the cut card size

Use **component trim size** for the game's original dimensions. The Japanese preset makes a 59 × 86 mm insert for a nominal 62 × 89 mm outer sleeve. For another size, choose **Custom card insert dimensions**, then enter the width and height in millimeters or inches. Forge stores the converted millimeters.

Sleeve packaging normally describes the outside of the sleeve. That is not a measurement of the card that fits inside it. Use a known card or a single test insert to establish the cut size; stock thickness and cutting affect fit. For example, 2.6 × 3.58 inches converts to 66.04 × 90.932 mm. This conversion does not establish that those dimensions fit a particular sleeve.

**Contain** preserves the entire face with space around it when proportions differ. **Cover** crops the edges. **Extend** preserves the face and fills the remaining area using the available bleed or painted edge. These home-print choices do not redefine component trim or the printer-service output.

## Choose paper and cuts

Both A4 and US Letter PDFs are available, in the saved portrait or landscape orientation. Paper choice changes how many cards fit on the sheet; it does not change the cut size. Match the PDF to the actual paper in your printer.

For guillotine cutting, choose **Shared cuts (no gaps)**. Forge sets the gutter to zero and places cut guides outside the grid. Choose **Each card corner** for a positive gutter. Changing the gutter keeps those two choices consistent. Choose **None** if you do not want cut guides.

Fronts-only sheets suit opaque sleeves. Duplex sheets mirror columns in portrait or rows in landscape for **flip on long edge**. Test registration on one sheet before printing the complete deck.

## Check the printer first

1. Save the reviewed settings.
2. Download the A4 or Letter calibration PDF matching your paper.
3. Print at **Actual size / 100%**. Disable Fit, Shrink, and borderless expansion.
4. Measure both rulers: each must be 50 mm. The PDF requests no scaling, but the printer dialog can override that request.
5. Cut the sample outline when present and test the sleeve. For larger inserts, use one card sheet after measuring the rulers.
6. Download and print the matching card sheets.

Calibration is a separate document, so it never becomes an extra card or an unpaired duplex page. Direct A4/Letter downloads use the saved insert choice. The full print ZIP also includes clearly named native-size and insert variants, the saved recipe, manifest, quantities, calibration sheets, and press handoffs.
